### What a deduction probably looks like for this product (inference)

- **Shortage and damage on delivery to a distributor's warehouse.** The proof
  is the signed BoL or PoD, the invoice and the PO. These are the documents
  the case page already reconciles.
- **Distributor compliance fees** (late, no-show, short fill), if McLane-style
  rules apply.
- **Unsaleables.** A bag-in-box mix with a shelf life.
- **Promotion and rebate claims.** Free Frazil Friday gives away a small cup
  "at the register"; no source says who pays for it. If Frazil reimburses the
  chain, that is a billback with a deal sheet behind it.
- **Price differences** after a price change, if distributors buy on a price
  list.

Machine service and repair are Frazil's own cost under the program and are
not deductions.

## 4. Frazil's accounting system

**Unknown.** No job post, vendor case study or filing read names it. A
company of this size and backing more often runs NetSuite, Sage Intacct or
Dynamics than QuickBooks Online, but that is a guess.

It matters because recouple's ledger leg reads QuickBooks Online only
(`packages/qbo`, ADR 0026); NetSuite is not built. If Frazil is not on
QuickBooks Online, the pilot runs on documents: upload, the workspace email
address, and a spreadsheet export of open deductions (ADR 0056). That is a
complete pilot, and it should be said on the call rather than discovered.

## 5. Questions for Shawn on Oct 14

Who pays you, and what they take:

1. Who do you invoice? Name the top five by revenue. Are they distributors,
   chains, schools, or a mix?
2. Which of them short-pay you, and roughly how many deductions and how many
   dollars a month does each take?
3. For each: how do you find out? A line on a remittance, a debit memo, a
   portal, an email, an EDI 812 or 820?
4. What reason codes do you see? Can you send one real remittance and one
   real deduction notice from each of the top three payers before the call?
5. Do you reimburse retailers for Free Frazil Friday or other promotions? If
   so, how is it claimed, and against what agreement?
6. Do any chains bill you directly (rebates, scan-based or volume programs),
   or does everything come through the distributor?

How you dispute today:

7. For each payer, where does a dispute go: a portal (which, and its
   address), an email address, a person?
8. Do you hold a login to McLane's supplier portal, Core-Mark's, or any
   other? Can the payer add a second, read-only user for us, or is there one
   shared login?
9. What does each payer ask you to attach? Signed BoL, PoD, invoice, PO, the
   promotion agreement, anything else?
10. What is each payer's deadline to dispute, and counted from what date?
    Where is that written (the supplier agreement, a vendor guide)? Can we
    have the document?
11. Has a payer ever reversed a deduction without your asking? What did that
    look like on the remittance?
12. What did you write off last year without disputing, and why?

Your documents and systems:

13. **What accounting system do you run** (QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks
    Desktop, NetSuite, Sage, Dynamics, other)? Who is the administrator?
14. Where do signed BoLs and PoDs live: with your carrier, your 3PL, a shared
    drive, the ERP? Are they filed by PO number?
15. Do you use an EDI provider (SPS Commerce, TrueCommerce, other), and which
    documents flow through it?
16. Where do remittances arrive: a shared mailbox, a lockbox, a portal
    download?
17. How do you code a deduction in the ledger today: one account, or one per
    reason?

Running the pilot:

18. Who on your side reviews a case, and who approves it? The product
    requires a second person to approve before anything is filed.
19. Who sends the dispute: you, from your own mailbox, or do you want us to
    prepare it for you to send?
20. What would make this worth it to you in the first 30 days: dollars back,
    hours saved, or knowing what you are losing?
